# Project Vision

Make *Nuns on the Run* a digital board game that people can enjoy on phones, tablets, laptops, and together around a 55-inch tabletop touchscreen, with private information on their phones.

The experience preserves the suspense of the original game: novices sneak through the abbey in pursuit of secret wishes while the Abbess and Prioress hunt for them. Players share the same unfolding game, but each knows only what their role allows. Risk, deduction, and surprise drive the play.

Around the tabletop, the game feels like a shared board game night. Players sit on every side, follow the action together, and keep their secrets close. The shared board draws attention back to the table and to one another.

On personal devices, the same game feels complete and comfortable, whether players gather in one room or play from different places. The choice of screen should make it easier for people to play together.

Success means players spend their time making decisions, following clues, and enjoying each other's reactions. The digital game takes care of the bookkeeping so the human game can take center stage.
